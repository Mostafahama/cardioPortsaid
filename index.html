— About the Conference —

A World-Class
Cardiac Summit

The 1st Cardio Portsaid Conference brings together Egypt's leading interventional cardiologists, electrophysiologists, cardiac surgeons, and allied health professionals for two days of cutting-edge science, live case presentations, and expert debates.

Under the presidency of Prof. Mohamed Imam, this inaugural conference sets a new benchmark for cardiac education in Port Said and the broader Egyptian medical community.
